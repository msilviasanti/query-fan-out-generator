What you're looking at. AI search engines like Google AI Mode split your question into many smaller searches, read the best passages, and write one answer. They don't show those searches to anyone. This tool writes the prompt that makes your AI assistant predict them, reasoning the same way. They're informed predictions, not data pulled from Google or any AI assistant.
